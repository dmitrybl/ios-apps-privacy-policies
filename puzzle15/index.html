Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzle 15 (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The App stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as:

  • selected game mode
  • selected tile design and theme
  • custom tile style settings
  • vibration preference
  • saved game progress
  • best scores

This local data is used only to provide app functionality and is not uploaded by us to our servers.

2. Advertising

The App uses third-party advertising services provided by Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and interstitial ads.

Advertising partners may process certain technical data to deliver and measure ads, which may include:

  • device identifiers
  • IP address
  • app/device information
  • approximate location inferred from IP
  • ad interaction data

This data is processed by the advertising provider under its own privacy policy.

Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

3. Analytics

We do not use separate analytics systems in the App beyond advertising-related measurement that may be performed by the ad SDK provider.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with third-party ad providers only as necessary to serve and improve advertising.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children directly. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will review the request.

6. Data Retention

Gameplay settings and progress are stored on your device and remain until you remove the App or clear app data from your device settings.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights related to your personal data (access, deletion, objection, etc.). For ad-related data processed by third-party providers, please refer to their privacy controls and policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures provided by the iOS platform and third-party SDKs, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the App Store listing or support page with a new effective date.
