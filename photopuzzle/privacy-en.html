Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dmitry Belyaev (“the developer”) handles information when you use Photo Puzzle: Around the World on iPhone or iPad.

1. Information We Handle

The App does not require an account. Unlocked levels, stars, best times, game settings, and information used to limit how often full-screen ads appear are stored on your device. The developer does not receive this local gameplay data.

In Quick Play, you can select an image using Apple’s photo picker or take a new photo with the camera. The App processes the image on your device to create a puzzle. It does not send the image to the developer or the advertising SDK. You can change camera access in your device settings. You can play the included levels without selecting a photo or using the camera.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to include. The developer uses that information to handle your request.

2. Advertising

The App uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and interstitial ads. Yandex may process device identifiers, advertising data, and technical information such as an IP address to deliver and measure ads. Processing depends on the SDK configuration and device permissions. The App does not request access to your location.

Yandex Privacy Policy · Yandex Mobile Ads data disclosures

3. Analytics

The developer does not use a separate analytics service. The advertising SDK includes AppMetrica components that may process app activity and diagnostic data as part of the advertising integration.

4. Data Sharing

Yandex receives information through its advertising SDK and processes it under its own privacy policy. Email services involved in delivering support messages handle those messages.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-audience game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact support so the developer can review the request.

6. Data Retention

Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device. The developer keeps support correspondence as long as needed to handle the request or as required by law. Yandex’s privacy policy describes how it handles data retention.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data or object to certain processing. Contact the developer about information you sent to support. For information handled by Yandex, use the contact options in its privacy policy.

8. Security

The App relies on iOS and iPadOS protections for local gameplay data and camera access. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

The developer will post updates on this page with a new effective date.
